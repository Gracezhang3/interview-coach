Describe a time you handled a conflict on a team. Okay, so in my second year we had a group project for our software design course. One teammate kept missing deadlines and, like, the rest of us were getting frustrated. Instead of complaining to the professor, I set up a quick one on one with him and it turned out he was dealing with a family issue. We re-split the work so he had smaller, more flexible tasks. We ended up submitting on time and actually got the highest mark in the section.
